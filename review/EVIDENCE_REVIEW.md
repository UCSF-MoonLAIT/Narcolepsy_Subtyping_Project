# Evidence-quality review

The 80 quote-flagged combined-pipeline outputs remain included in primary classification metrics when structurally valid and metadata matched. Quote fidelity and diagnostic correctness are separate outcomes. Strict sensitivity requires supported quotes in both pipelines.

The [methods narrative](REPORT.md#evidence-flags-and-evaluation-eligibility) specifies the flagging rules, and its [source-text audit section](REPORT.md#source-text-quality-audit) describes how rejected quotations were examined. Automated candidate matches were checked against source text; this was a source-fidelity audit rather than independent clinical adjudication.

All 80 outputs and 125 rejected quotations were examined. Case-level categories: 31 priority evidence reviews, 38 other rewrites or joined quotations, 9 capitalization/punctuation-only cases, and 2 spelling/split-word-spacing-only cases. Of 277 quotes, 152 passed source matching and 125 were rejected. Seventy-four cases had at least one accepted quote. Model/reference agreement was 46/80; this selected-subset statistic is not clinical adjudication.

No new specialist clinical adjudication is included in this repository review; clinical confirmation and reviewer-decision fields remain blank because that work was not performed. Detailed patient-level audit files are in the restricted appendix.
